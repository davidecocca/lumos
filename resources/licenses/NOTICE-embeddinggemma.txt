Lumos bundles the EmbeddingGemma 300M embedding model (ONNX, q4 quantization) to
provide offline semantic search over notes.

  Model:      google/embeddinggemma-300m
  ONNX build: onnx-community/embeddinggemma-300m-ONNX (pinned revision,
              see scripts/embedding-model.lock.json)
  Source:     https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/embeddinggemma-300m-ONNX

The model weights are made available under the Google Gemma Terms of Use:

  https://ai.google.dev/gemma/terms

Use of the model is subject to those terms, including the Gemma Prohibited Use
Policy (https://ai.google.dev/gemma/prohibited_use_policy). This notice file is
included with the application and with every installer that ships the model.
